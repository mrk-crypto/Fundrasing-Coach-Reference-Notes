重新框架 · Reframe

Fundraising is not "asking for money" — it is inviting others into God's mission. Supporters invest in God's work, not in you personally. This is partnership, not charity.

复制

根本原因 · Root Cause
华人文化中的「人情」观念；害怕关系破裂；不愿成为别人"负担"的集体主义价值观。
The Chinese concept of relational debt (人情); fear of rupturing relationships; collectivist value of not burdening others.
重新框架 · Reframe
真正的朋友希望参与你的旅程。邀请别人是给他们选择权——说不是可以的，一次拒绝不会毁掉真正的关系。拒绝的是时机，不是你这个人。
True friends want to be part of your journey. Inviting gives them a choice — saying no is okay. One refusal does not ruin a genuine relationship. They are declining the timing, not rejecting you.
教练提问 · Coaching Questions

Theological Framework · 神学框架
Core Framework · 核心框架
募款不是向人要钱，而是向人发出邀请——邀请他们成为神使命的伙伴。
使命属于神，工人是管家，支持者是同工。这个框架改变了一切：工人开口的身份、支持者参与的角色、以及那次邀请本身的意义。
Fundraising is not asking people for money — it is inviting them to become partners in God's mission. The mission belongs to God; workers are stewards; supporters are co-workers. This framework changes everything: the worker's identity when asking, the supporter's role in giving, and the meaning of the invitation itself.
Three Theological Pillars · 三根神学支柱
01
使命属于神，工人是管家 The Mission Belongs to God — Workers Are Stewards
Identity Theological
▼
神学核心 · Theological Core
工人不是在为"自己的项目"募款。他们是神使命的管家，被呼召来推进一件不属于他们、却需要他们的工作。神拥有这个使命，也拥有所有的资源；工人的任务是邀请人参与神已经在做的事。

Workers are not fundraising for "their own project." They are stewards of God's mission — called to advance work that does not belong to them but needs them. God owns the mission and all resources; the worker's task is to invite people into what God is already doing.
经文锚点 · Scripture Anchor
路加福音 Luke 10:2
「庄稼多，工人少，所以你们当求庄稼的主，打发工人出去收祂的庄稼。」
"The harvest is plentiful, but the workers are few. Ask the Lord of the harvest, therefore, to send out workers into his harvest field."
路加福音 Luke 10:7
「工人得工价是应当的。」
"The worker deserves his wages."
对应障碍 · Addresses
▸ 身份认同障碍（"我是在为自己要钱"）
▸ 神学障碍（"不确定神的呼召"）
▸ 神学障碍（"神会自己预备，不需要我开口"）
教练语言 · Coaching Language
「你不是在为自己的项目募款——你是在邀请人参与神正在做的事。」"You are not fundraising for your own project — you are inviting people into what God is already doing."
「神呼召你，神也会预备你。祂预备的方式，就是通过祂的百姓。」"God called you; God will provide for you. His way of providing is through His people."
「开口寻求支持，本身就是一个信心的动作——因为你在相信神会通过别人来成就。」"Seeking support is itself an act of faith — you are trusting that God will work through others."
02
邀请是礼物，不是负担 The Invitation is a Gift — Not a Burden
Identity Relational
▼
神学核心 · Theological Core
每一次邀请，都给支持者一个机会：让他们的资源与永恒的使命连接。保罗告诉腓立比教会，他们的支持在神的账上产生了"果实"——给予者受益，不只是接受者。工人发出邀请，是在给对方一份礼物，不是在麻烦他们。

Every invitation gives supporters an opportunity: to connect their resources to an eternal mission. Paul told the Philippian church their support produced "fruit credited to their account" — the giver benefits, not just the receiver. The worker's invitation is a gift to the supporter, not a burden.
经文锚点 · Scripture Anchor
腓立比书 Philippians 4:17
「我所求的，并不是馈送，所求的乃是你们所结的果子，可以归在你们的账上，越来越多。」
"Not that I desire your gifts; what I desire is that more be credited to your account."
对应障碍 · Addresses
▸ 身份认同障碍（"开口=乞讨"）
▸ 关系障碍（"不想麻烦朋友"）
▸ 关系障碍（"怕给对方负担"）
教练语言 · Coaching Language
「你的邀请是在给对方一个机会——让他们的生命与神的使命连接。」"Your invitation gives them an opportunity — to connect their lives to God's mission."
「说不是可以的。你给的是邀请，不是压力；结果由他们和神之间决定。」"Saying no is okay. You are giving an invitation, not applying pressure; the outcome is between them and God."
「保罗说支持者的给予在他们的账上产生了果实——你的邀请让这成为可能。」"Paul said supporters' gifts credited fruit to their own account — your invitation makes that possible for them."
03
差遣与被差遣，都是使命的一部分 Sending and Going Are Both Part of the Mission
Relational Theological
▼
神学核心 · Theological Core
使命需要两种人：去的人和差遣的人。支持者不是使命的边缘人，他们是使命的参与者。他们的财务支持本身就是一种服事。工人邀请支持者，不是在"拿走"他们的资源，而是在邀请他们"进入"一场他们也属于其中的使命。

The mission needs two kinds of people: those who go and those who send. Supporters are not peripheral to the mission — they are participants in it. Financial support is itself a form of ministry. Workers invite supporters not to "take" their resources but to "bring them into" a mission they also belong to.
经文锚点 · Scripture Anchor
罗马书 Romans 10:14-15
「他们未曾信的，怎能求祂呢？未曾听见祂，怎能信呢？没有传道的，怎能听见呢？若没有奉差遣，怎能传道呢？」
"How can they believe in the one of whom they have not heard? And how can they hear without someone preaching to them? And how can anyone preach unless they are sent?"
对应障碍 · Addresses
▸ 关系障碍（"不想麻烦关系好的人"）
▸ 神学障碍（"使命不够重要"）
▸ 身份认同障碍（"依赖别人是软弱"）
教练语言 · Coaching Language
「你的支持者不是在'资助'你——他们是在'差遣'你，他们也是使命的一部分。」"Your supporters are not 'funding' you — they are 'sending' you. They too are part of the mission."
「没有被差遣的人，传道的人就无法出去。你让他们有机会成为罗马书10章里那个'差遣的人'。」"Without senders, those who preach cannot go. You give them the chance to be the 'sender' in Romans 10."
「每一位支持你的人，将来都能说：'我也参与了那个工作。'」"Every supporter will one day be able to say: 'I was part of that work too.'"
Key Scriptures · 核心经文参考
腓 4:15–17
「你们腓立比人也知道……在馈送和交接的事上，与我有来往的，就只有你们……你们所结的果子，可以归在你们的账上，越来越多。」
"…No church shared with me in giving and receiving, except you only… what I desire is that more be credited to your account."
伙伴关系的神学 — Theology of partnership: supporters share in the fruit
路 10:7
「工人得工价是应当的。」
"The worker deserves his wages." (Luke 10:7)
工人被支持的神圣合法性 — Divine legitimacy of workers being supported
罗 10:14–15
「若没有奉差遣，怎能传道呢？」
"How can anyone preach unless they are sent?" (Romans 10:15)
差遣的神学 — Theology of sending: supporters are essential co-workers
腓 4:19
「我的神必照祂荣耀的丰富，在基督耶稣里，使你们一切所需用的都充足。」
"My God will supply every need of yours according to his riches in glory in Christ Jesus." (Phil 4:19)
神是终极供应者 — God as ultimate provider; fundraising is trusting Him
林后 9:6–7
「少种的少收，多种的多收……各人要随本心所酌定的，不要作难，不要勉强；因为捐得乐意的人是神所喜爱的。」
"Whoever sows generously will also reap generously… God loves a cheerful giver." (2 Cor 9:6–7)
给予是蒙福的 — Giving is a spiritual blessing, not just a financial transaction
